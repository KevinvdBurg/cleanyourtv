Retrieved on 24 October 2026:

- `primevideo.svg`: [Prime Video logo (2024).svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prime_Video_logo_(2024).svg) on Wikimedia Commons, public domain as a text logo, trademarked.
- `disneyplus.svg`: [disneyplus](https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/504281/disneyplus) on SVG Repo. Its page sat behind a bot check, so its licence was not recorded here.

HBO Max and Disney+ use group transforms and shapes; their drawables have those flattened into plain paths, with the same outlines.

The Android drawables preserve these paths, use white fills for the launcher's tile treatment and keep each logo's aspect ratio, with the viewport cropped to the logo. Dropout has 25% smaller artwork within its viewport to balance its visual weight against the other dock logos. Source SVGs retain their original colours. Brand marks belong to their respective owners; the Simple Icons CC0 license in the app's assets applies to the Simple Icons sources, not these files.
