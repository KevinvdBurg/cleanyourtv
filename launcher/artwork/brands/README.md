# Brand SVG sources

Original SVG files retrieved on 21 October 2026:

- `dropout.svg`: Dropout's standalone mark, linked from [dropout.tv](https://www.dropout.tv/). [Original SVG](https://dropout-hub.cdn.prismic.io/dropout-hub/aK8ppmGNHVfTOX-v_Dropout_Logo_Bug_Black.svg).
- `skyshowtime.svg`: the inline `skyshowtime-wob-logo` SVG from the navigation on [SkyShowtime's Dutch website](https://www.skyshowtime.com/nl).

The Android drawables preserve these paths, use white fills for the launcher's tile treatment and keep each logo's aspect ratio. Dropout has 25% smaller artwork within its viewport to balance its visual weight against the other dock logos. Source SVGs retain their original colours. Brand marks belong to their respective owners; the Simple Icons CC0 license in the app's assets applies to the Simple Icons sources, not these two files.
